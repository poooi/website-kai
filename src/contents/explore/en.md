## About poi

poi is an open-source KanColle browser for Windows, macOS and Linux, built with Electron and React. Plugins extend its built-in tools to suit the way you play.

## Features

- Take screenshots, reload or mute the game, and adjust its resolution.
- Configure HTTP, SOCKS5 or PAC proxies and forward game data.
- View admiral details, resources, fleets and land-based air squadrons at a glance.
- Track expeditions, repairs, construction and quests. Detailed quest descriptions are provided by plugins.
- Get notifications for construction, expeditions, repairs and fatigue recovery. Check ship and equipment capacity and ship lock status before sortieing.
- Customize the layout, theme, icons and ship portraits.
- Use the interface in multiple languages.
- Build extensions with access to game data and the plugin API.
